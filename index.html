The Story

Woven from tradition

The tibeb is the soul of Habesha fashion — geometric borders woven with silk threads on handlooms, each pattern a story passed through generations.

The art of the handloom

From the white cotton of the highlands to the vibrant threads of celebration, every Habesha kemis honors its origin while meeting the elegance of modern life.

We work directly with master weavers across Ethiopia, ensuring each garment is authentic, handcrafted, and alive with heritage.
